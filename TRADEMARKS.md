## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may:

- refer truthfully to the RefKit project or official RefKit releases;
- state that your software is compatible with, integrates with, or is based on RefKit, when the statement is accurate;
- link to the official RefKit website or repository;
- use reasonable screenshots for news, commentary, education, or documentation; and
- redistribute an unmodified official release while accurately identifying it as unmodified.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement, or operation by RefKit.

## Modified versions and forks

A materially modified distribution must use a distinct product name and visual identity. It may say "based on RefKit" or "compatible with RefKit" in a factual, secondary way. Do not use RefKit logos or a confusingly similar name as the fork's primary brand, application icon, domain, organization name, or package name.

Removing RefKit branding from a modified distribution does not change the software license or any AGPL Corresponding Source obligation.

## Permission required

Written permission is required for uses such as:

- presenting a modified service as an official RefKit service;
- using RefKit marks in a company, product, domain, event, or commercial service name;
- selling merchandise that carries RefKit marks; or
- altering or combining RefKit logos as a primary identity.

Contact `support@refkit.net` to request permission. Permission is not granted until confirmed in writing.
