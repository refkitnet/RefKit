# Community code of conduct

RefKit aims to provide a constructive, safe, and professional project community.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- be respectful and specific, especially during disagreement;
- critique ideas and changes rather than people;
- welcome questions asked in good faith;
- respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information; and
- accept reasonable moderation and move on when a decision is final.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, impersonation, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Abuse of project channels for spam, scams, credential collection, or coordinated harassment is also prohibited.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project events, and other spaces where a person represents the RefKit project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@refkit.net`. Do not include sensitive personal information beyond what is needed to assess the report. If the report concerns a maintainer, identify that conflict so another maintainer can handle it where possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Enforcement considers context, severity, pattern, and community safety. Confidentiality will be respected where practical, but absolute confidentiality or a particular outcome cannot be promised.

Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), not the conduct channel.
